# WindBoy 

## Day-1
1st November, i wanted to build a useful project for my home since i live in India there is there are coming unexpected rain or winds and i wanted to check the weather condition while sitting inside chilling so i came a idea of making a thing which could measure wind speed, pressure, temperature, humidity, AQI level, and rain and it could be controlled by another device with almost plenty of range that's why i came with the project **WindBoy** it will use two esp32 and a protocol named ESP-NOW and will use many environment sensors This is my day-1 idea
